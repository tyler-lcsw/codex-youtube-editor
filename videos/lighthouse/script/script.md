# The Little Robot Who Brought the Light Back

Every evening, Pip lit the little lighthouse. Its warm beam guided the last boat home.

But tonight, the harbor went dark. Pip pressed the switch again. Nothing. Out on the water, a tiny light was waiting.

Pip stopped guessing and followed the signal. The battery was full. The lamp was ready. Between them, one connection had come loose.

With one careful click, Pip joined both ends. Then came the longest second of the night.

The beacon swept across the waves. The boat answered, and turned toward home. Pip did not need more power. Just one small connection.
