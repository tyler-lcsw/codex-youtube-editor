Precision Cut

Three color families. A precise cut. A calm workspace for turning footage into a story.

The palette

Ivory
#F5EBDD
Coral
#F36B4F
Graphite
#303234

Ivory supplies warmth and space; graphite supplies structure and text; coral marks intentional action. The editable vector preserves the selected split-film idea, three perforations per piece, clean transparent corners and precise colors. Its reduced shading is intentional for small icons.

Text and controls use tonal variations within these families: deep coral #A93420 on light surfaces, soft coral #FF947C on dark surfaces. Measured contrast: graphite/ivory 10.92:1, deep coral/ivory 5.57:1, soft coral/graphite 5.99:1, white/deep-coral button 6.56:1. These are token-pair measurements, not a claim of complete app accessibility conformance.

Application rules

  • Keep coral sparse: actions, selection cues and annotation emphasis.
  • Use text and shape to communicate state. A coral badge does not mean approval.
  • Use native control symbols; custom artwork belongs to the application identity.
  • Keep video content free of a global color treatment. Production branding belongs to each video project.
  • Honor macOS light/dark appearance and preserve native keyboard, focus and disabled behavior.

Maintaining the assets

Edit macos/Brand/PrecisionCut.svg for the icon and macos/Sources/Studio/StudioTheme.swift for UI tokens. Run .venv/bin/python tools/build_studio_icons.py on macOS with librsvg installed, then rebuild the application. Routine app builds use committed resources and need no image-generation service or additional renderer.
